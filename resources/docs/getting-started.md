# Getting Started & Platform Overview

Welcome to **KDAnalytiks**, an end-to-end research, questionnaire design, data collection and AI-powered statistical analysis platform.

---

## Key Features & Capabilities

KDAnalytiks combines modern survey design with advanced statistical intelligence and AI analysis. Whether you are conducting academic research, university coursework or market research campaigns, KDAnalytiks provides:

- **Visual Drag-and-Drop Builder**: Build complex questionnaires with 12+ question types, branching logic and custom validation.
- **AI Questionnaire Architect**: Generate complete survey drafts instantly using natural language prompts or pre-built industry templates.
- **Multi-Channel Fieldwork**: Deploy surveys via web links, QR codes, embed widgets or targeted Email/SMS campaigns.
- **Rewarded Surveys & Wallet System**: Fund cash incentives for respondents to boost response rates with automated fraud detection and direct wallet payouts.
- **Collaborative Analysis Groups**: Divide researchers, students, or team members into isolated groups that share report metrics while keeping AI chat discussions private.
- **Socius AI Assistant & RAG**: Query your live survey dataset in natural language, generate executive summaries, build cross-tabulations and feed custom literature PDFs into the AI knowledge base.
- **Academic & Research Tools**: Draft research proposals, generate literature review structures, format citations (APA/IEEE) and publish datasets.

---

## User Roles & Accounts

KDAnalytiks supports three distinct user roles tailored to different research needs:

| Account Role               | Target Audience                                                    | Primary Capabilities                                                                                |
| :------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Independent Researcher** | Individual researchers, students and consultants                   | Create surveys, run AI analysis, collect responses, export report packages and manage wallets.      |
| **Organization**           | Academic institutions, departments, research firms and enterprises | Multi-user team management, shared survey assets, high-volume response limits and custom branding.  |
| **Respondent**             | Survey participants and panel members                              | Complete surveys, earn cash rewards for paid questionnaires and withdraw earnings to local wallets. |

## Account Setup & Navigation

### 1. Registration & Profile Configuration

- Register as an **Independent Researcher** or **Organization**.
- Verify your email address to unlock full survey publishing and AI analysis features.
- Navigate to **Account Settings** to update your display name, institution affiliation and contact details.

### 2. Interface Language & Locale Switching

KDAnalytiks supports full UI localization in 7 languages:

- **English** (🇬🇧)
- **Kiswahili** (🇰🇪)
- **Français** (🇫🇷)
- **Deutsch** (🇩🇪)
- **Español** (🇪🇸)
- **Arabic** (🇸🇦)
- **Chinese** (🇨🇳)

To switch your language, click the language flag selector in the top navigation bar at any time.
