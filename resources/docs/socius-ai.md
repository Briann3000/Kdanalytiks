# Socius AI Assistant

**Socius** is your AI analysis partner designed to help you analyze, synthesize and query survey data in natural language.

---

## 1. What can Socius do?

- **Executive Summary**: Generates structured, high-level summaries of all responses instantly.
- **Granular Querying**: Ask questions like "Summarize the complaints from users aged 18-25" or "Synthesize the open-ended text answers."
- **Memory & Guidelines**: Set stylistic guidelines and guidelines (such as target formats, tone or constraints) that Socius will remember across your analysis sessions.
- **Visual Synthesis**: Request Socius to generate custom charts, flowcharts or images based on response statistics.

---

## 2. Natural Language Dataset Q&A

Under the **Analyse** tab in your survey reports, open the Socius chat panel to ask questions about your data: -_For Example:_

- _"What are the top three concerns raised by respondents in Section B?"_
- _"Compare the satisfaction ratings between male and female respondents."_
- _"Generate a 200-word executive summary of the overall findings."_

---

## 3. Interactive Crosstabs & Statistical Queries

Socius can generate cross-tabulations and frequency breakdowns on demand:

- Ask Socius to generate crosstabs between demographic variables and outcome scores.
- Request hypothesis suggestions or key statistical takeaways.

---

## 4. Thread Management & Group Isolation

- Create multiple chat threads to explore different research topics.
- For surveys using **Analysis Groups**, chat threads are completely isolated per group, ensuring student teams or research groups work independently.
