# AI Architect & Template Library

Accelerate survey creation using the **AI Questionnaire Architect** or choose from pre-built, industry-standard research templates.

---

## The AI Questionnaire Architect

The **AI Architect** transforms high-level research objectives into structured questionnaires within seconds.

### How to Use the AI Architect

1. Click **+ New Survey** on your dashboard and select **AI Architect**.
2. Describe your survey objective in plain language (e.g., _"Design a 10-question customer satisfaction survey for a mobile banking app in East Africa"_).
3. Specify optional parameters:
   - **Target Audience**: (e.g., University Students, Mobile App Users, Business Owners).
   - **Question Count**: (e.g., 5, 10, 15, or 20 questions).
   - **Scale Preference**: Likert scale, open-ended, or mixed format.
4. Click **Generate Draft**. The AI will generate a complete survey structure with titles, categories, questions, options and recommended scale types.

## Pre-Built Template Library

KDAnalytiks includes a library of validated research templates:

- **Academic & Education**: Course Evaluation, Student Satisfaction, Thesis Research Data Collection.
- **Market Research & Business**: Customer Satisfaction (CSAT), Product Feedback, Brand Awareness, NPS Evaluation.
- **Human Resources**: Employee Engagement, Workplace Climate, 360-Degree Feedback.
- **Healthcare & Public Health**: Patient Experience, Community Health Assessment, Service Evaluation.

### Using a Template

1. Navigate to **Survey Templates** from the survey creation menu.
2. Filter by category or search by topic.
3. Click **Preview Template** to view the question sequence.
4. Select **Use Template** to copy it into your survey builder workspace.
