# Branding & Themes

Customize your survey's visual aesthetic to align with your organization's brand identity, university logo, or research institutional guidelines.

---

## 1. Visual Styling & Color Themes

In the Survey Builder, click the **Theme & Styling (🎨)** tab to access visual customization options:

- **Pre-Set Color Palettes**: Choose from curated color schemes (e.g. Modern Indigo, Emerald Academic, Classic Navy, Dark Mode Slate).
- **Custom Brand Colors**: Enter your institution's exact HEX or HSL color codes for primary accents, buttons and background colors.
- **Typography**: Select modern Google Fonts (Inter, Roboto, Outfit, Playfair Display) for survey headings and body text.

---

## 2. Logo Upload & Headers

Add institutional authority to your questionnaires:

- Upload high-resolution PNG or SVG logos.
- Position logos in top-left, center, or top-right header locations.
- Adjust logo height and padding.
