# Paid Surveys & Wallet Incentives

Boost response rates and incentivize quality feedback by attaching cash reward budgets to your surveys.

---

## 1. Setting Up a Rewarded Survey

1. Go to **Survey Settings** -> **Reward Budget**.
2. Toggle **Enable Cash Rewards**.
3. Set the **Reward Amount per Response** (e.g. KES 50 or $0.50 per completed submission).
4. Specify the **Total Response Cap**.
5. Fund the total reward budget from your KDAnalytiks balance.

---

## 2. Respondent Wallet & Payout System

When respondents complete a paid survey, earnings are automatically credited to their KDAnalytiks Wallet.

- **Balance Tracking**: Respondents view earned rewards in their personal dashboard wallet.
- **Withdrawal Requests**: Respondents can withdraw earnings directly to local mobile money accounts (e.g., M-PESA) or bank accounts.
- **Payout Approval & Log**: Survey owners can inspect payout ledgers and transaction history.

---

## 3. Data Quality & Anti-Fraud Protection

To ensure reward funds are spent on genuine data, KDAnalytiks applies multi-layered automated fraud checks:

- **Speeder Detection**: Submissions completed faster than a minimum threshold are flagged as invalid.
- **Straightlining Detection**: Submissions selecting identical scale values across matrix grids are flagged.
- **Duplicate IP & Device Fingerprinting**: Prevents single users from submitting multiple entries on different accounts.
- **Bot Mitigation & CAPTCHA**: Automatically prompts suspicious traffic with bot checks.
