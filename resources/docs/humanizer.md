# AI Research Humanizer

The **AI Research Humanizer** refines automated AI text outputs into polished, academic, natural human writing suitable for research papers, thesis chapters and formal reporting.

---

## Features & Capabilities

- **Academic Tone Refinement**: Eliminates repetitive AI phrasing (e.g. _"in conclusion", "it is important to note"_) and replaces them with scholarly prose.
- **Fluency & Clarity**: Enhances readability while preserving precise technical terminology and numerical data.
- **Custom Tone Presets**: Choose between _Formal Academic_, _Casual_ or _Creative_.

---

## How to Use the Humanizer

1. Navigate to **AI Humanizer** from the sidebar or from Socius.
2. Paste your draft paragraph, AI summary or report section into the input canvas.
3. Select your target tone preset.
4. Click **Humanize Text**.
5. Compare the original and refined output side-by-side.
