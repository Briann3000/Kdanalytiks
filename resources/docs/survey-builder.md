# Survey Builder & Question Types

The **Survey Builder** is an intuitive, drag-and-drop workspace for designing professional questionnaires, academic instruments and feedback forms.

---

## Core Question Types

KDAnalytiks supports 12+ question types designed for qualitative, quantitative and multimedia data collection:

### 1. Multiple Choice & Checkboxes

- **Single Select**: Ideal for mutually exclusive choices (e.g., Age Range, Gender, Primary Occupation).
- **Multi-Select**: Allows respondents to select all options that apply. Supports minimum and maximum selection limits.

### 2. Rating & Matrix Scales

- **Likert Scale**: Standard 5-point or 7-point agreement scales (_Strongly Disagree_ to _Strongly Agree_).
- **Matrix Grid**: Evaluate multiple sub-items or statements using a unified scale grid to keep surveys concise.
- **Star & Numeric Rating**: Interactive star rating (1–5 or 1–10) for satisfaction or quality scores.

### 3. Text & Open-Ended Input

- **Short Text**: Single-line text input for names, short answers, or codes.
- **Textarea**: Multi-line text box for detailed qualitative responses and comments.

### 4. Special & Multimedia Input

- **Audio & Video Upload**: Enables respondents to record or upload voice notes and video feedback directly.
- **File Upload**: Collect documents, images, or receipts with file extension and size restrictions.
- **Digital Signature**: Collect handwritten digital signatures for consent forms and official declarations.
- **Geographic Location Picker**: Captures GPS coordinates or map locations for field studies and location audit.
- **Date & Time Picker**: Standard calendar date and time selectors.

---

## Question Settings & Validation Rules

Click on any question card in the builder to open its settings drawer:

- **Mandatory / Required Flag**: Prevent survey submission if the question is left unanswered.
- **Min/Max Selection Constraints**: Set exact limits on checkbox selections.
- **Regex & Text Format Validation**: Enforce email address formats, phone number patterns, or numeric ranges.
- **Help Text & Sub-Instructions**: Add secondary descriptions or context below the question title.
