# Deployment & Invite Campaigns

Deploy your surveys across multiple channels to reach your target respondents efficiently.

---

## 1. Distribution Channels

Once a survey is published, navigate to the **Deploy** tab to access collection methods:

- **Direct Web Link**: A clean, shareable URL to send via messaging apps or social media.
- **QR Code Generation**: Download high-resolution PNG/SVG QR codes for print posters, flyers, or classroom slides.
- **Website Embed Code**: Copy responsive `iframe` HTML snippets to embed the survey directly into your website or portal.

---

## 2. Targeted Invite Campaigns (Email & SMS)

For formal studies or organizational feedback where respondent identities must be verified, use **Invite Campaigns**.

### Features of Invite Campaigns

- **Bulk Recipient Import**: Upload recipient lists via CSV or Excel containing email addresses and phone numbers.
- **Personalized Single-Use Tokens**: Every recipient receives a unique link that can only be submitted once, eliminating duplicate responses.
- **Custom Email & SMS Templates**: Customize email subject lines, body text and sender branding.
- **Campaign Tracking**: Monitor delivery status (Sent, Opened, Completed, Bounced) in real-time.
- **Automated Reminders**: Send follow-up reminder messages to uncompleted recipients with one click.
