# Research Proposal Builder

Draft academic research proposals, construct problem statements, outline methodology frameworks and generate citations automatically using the **Research Proposal Builder**.

---

## 1. Structured Proposal Generation

The **Research Proposal Builder** assists students, faculty and institutional researchers in drafting formal proposals:

- **Title & Background Generator**: Develop clear research titles and background context based on your domain.
- **Problem Statement Formulation**: Structure compelling problem statements with clear research gaps.
- **Research Questions & Objectives**: Formulate primary and secondary research questions aligned with SMART objectives.
- **Methodology Outliner**: Draft sampling techniques, target population criteria and data collection procedures.

---

## 2. Citation & Reference Generator

Generate properly formatted reference lists in multiple academic styles:

- **APA 7th Edition**
- **IEEE Standard**
- **Harvard Style**
- **MLA Format**

Save reference libraries directly within your KDAnalytiks account to export alongside your survey findings.
