# Academic Publications

Share, cite and publish completed survey research datasets and executive reports to the academic community.

---

## 1. Publishing Findings

Once data collection and analysis are complete, you can publish your findings on KDAnalytiks:

- **Public Research Directory**: Make your summary report discoverable by other scholars and institutions.
- **DOI Assignment**: Assign Digital Object Identifiers (DOIs) to published reports for persistent academic citation.
- **Open Access Data Sharing**: Choose whether to publish aggregated summary charts, anonymized raw datasets, or restricted executive briefs.

---

## 2. Citing Datasets

Every published survey on KDAnalytiks generates a standard academic citation block (APA/IEEE) so other researchers can cite your dataset correctly in their literature reviews.
